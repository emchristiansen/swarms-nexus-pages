Theseus private reports

Theseus private reports is a personal reporting tool for the owner of the Theseus agent system. The calling agent writes the report in a separate continuation of its existing conversation, and the tool places the report in the owner's dedicated Gmail inbox. The receiving account is controlled by the owner; signing in to that account stays on the owner's device.

Google authorization gives this app permission to insert a new message into that inbox and confirm the receiving account's identity. It does not request permission to read existing mail, delete mail, or send mail to other recipients. The requested permissions are Gmail insertion, OpenID, and email identity.

The reporting tool is still being prepared and is switched off. A Google app's publishing status does not switch on reporting. Consent, installation, and any real delivery require their own completed setup.

Privacy has limits. Google receives each inserted report. The reporting agent's AI provider processes the available conversation context and resulting report before delivery, and the shared workstation and agent transcripts can expose content or the fact that reporting occurred. This tool does not yet provide isolation from other agents or users on that workstation.

Read our privacy policy before authorizing access: https://swarms.nexus/private-reports/privacy.html .

For setup questions, contact theseus@swarms.nexus. This is a shared support mailbox: do not send a private report, password, client secret, or authorization token there.
